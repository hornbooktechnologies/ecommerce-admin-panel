WAREHOUSE TRANSFERS - REVIEW AND SAFEGUARDS

Location: Inventory > Transfer on an available stock balance.
Implemented in D:\Global-ecommerce\ecommerce-admin-panel.

Flow
1. Select another active warehouse for the same seller.
2. Enter quantity and, when required, source batch; or select exact serials.
3. Supply transfer notes and review source, destination, product/SKU, quantity,
   batch/serial identity, expected source balance and destination increase.
4. Edit without losing inputs, or Confirm transfer.
Review sends no stock-changing request.

Rules retained
Existing backend endpoint, SQL/transaction rules and permissions are unchanged.
The transfer is immediate, not dispatch/in-transit/receipt management.
Reserved and quarantined units cannot move; serialized transfers retain their
individual identities and associated batch traceability.
Non-serialized quantities support six decimals; serial selections are capped at
the lesser of available stock, available serials and the API's 500-item limit.
Serial picker displays 10 items per page and preserves cross-page selections.

Safeguards added
- Explicit destination/batch selection; no auto-selection of the first warehouse.
- Review before posting; unchanged input survives token refresh.
- Unsaved-close confirmation and browser reload warning where supported.
- Double-click lock during preflight and posting.
- Read-only refresh of stock and eligible warehouses before POST.
- Changed stock/reservations/tracking, selected serial identity/availability or
  destination details require a new review. User input is retained.
- Unavailable selected serials remain visible for explicit removal.
- A failed preflight sends no transfer. Ambiguous POST failures warn to check
  both warehouses/history before retrying; no automatic retry or idempotency added.
- Preflight is not an atomic expected-version check. Stock can change between
  preflight and POST; the existing transaction enforces current availability.
- Tenant-keyed dialogs and unmount checks prevent old requests from closing a new
  workspace's form. Already-sent server writes may still complete for their tenant.
- Body portal, internal scrolling, keyboard containment, focused errors/headings,
  wrapped controls and theme/reduced-motion styles follow UI/UX guidance.

Scope
No dependencies, migration or backend changes. No actual stock was transferred.
Receive stock, Count stock, policy and warehouse creation flows are retained.
No autosave, arbitrary SPA-navigation blocker or in-transit tracking introduced.

Verification
Source/diff review only. Lint, tests, build and browser checks not run.
Manual verification pending:
- Quantity, decimal, batch and serial transfers; source/destination shown correctly.
- Wrong seller/same warehouse/inactive destination excluded.
- Quantity above source or batch availability, blank notes, zero serials.
- Serial page navigation and selection retention, 500 limit and unavailable removal.
- Review/edit and Cancel/X/Escape/backdrop unsaved guards.
- Token refresh and tenant switch while editing/preflight/posting.
- Stock or destination change before confirmation requires rereview, no POST.
- Preflight error, successful transfer, ambiguous network outcome guidance.
- Mobile/landscape, light/dark, long serial labels, keyboard and reduced motion.
